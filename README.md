## Project Overview

Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market, selling products such as tomatoes, onions, sukuma, potatoes, and avocados. She needs to understand her sales performance, identify strong and weak trading days, monitor stock levels, and make better restocking decisions. This project uses Python to analyse 14 days of sales data and evaluate product stock levels and gross profit margins, helping Wanjiku make informed business decisions.

## How to Run


Open the notebook in Google Colab: [Open in Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/Be.../stall_analysis.ipynb)

## Top Three Findings

1. **Sales Growth:** Weekly sales increased from KES 22,050 in Week 1 to KES 24,400 in Week 2, representing **10.66% growth**.
2. **Weekend Performance:** Average weekend sales were KES 4,900, which was **82.50% higher** than the weekday average of KES 2,685.
3. **Stock Management:** Tomatoes (18 kg) and sukuma (9 kg) were below their reorder levels and should be restocked.
